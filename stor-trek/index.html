How It Works

Seven simple steps from drop-off to retrieval. We handle the heavy lifting — literally.

1
🚗
Arrive

Pull up to a Stor Trek facility with your items. No appointment needed for drop-off, or schedule in advance via the app.

2
📸
Intake

Our team photographs every item, documents any pre-existing damage, and creates a detailed digital record. You sign off.

3
🛡️
Protect

Add padding wraps, boxes, packing supplies, or item insurance at intake. We offer protection options for every budget.

4
🏢
Store

Items go into our climate-controlled facility, each assigned a unique location code. Billing starts per your chosen plan.

5
💻
Manage

Log into your Stor Trek dashboard to view all stored items with photos, current costs, and billing plans — anytime.

6
📅
Schedule

Select any item from your profile and book a pickup with your preferred date and time window. We'll have it ready.

7
📦
Retrieve

Show up, we load your items. Billing stops the moment the item leaves. You get a full receipt for the storage period.

Questions

Frequently Asked

How is this different from a regular storage unit?
Traditional storage charges you for square footage — whether you fill it or not. Stor Trek charges per item. You only pay for what you actually store, and we handle all the loading, unloading, and organization.
What happens when I want my item back?
Log into your dashboard, select the item, and schedule a pickup time. Our staff will have everything ready when you arrive. Billing stops the moment your item leaves the facility.
What if my item gets damaged while in storage?
Every item is photographed and damage-documented at intake. We note all pre-existing damage before it enters our care. Optional item protection plans are available for additional coverage.
Can I store any type of item?
We store most household and commercial items — furniture, appliances, boxes, electronics, bikes, and specialty items like pianos and gym equipment. Hazardous materials are not accepted.
Is the facility climate-controlled?
Yes. All Stor Trek facilities are fully climate-controlled to protect your items from temperature and humidity damage year-round.
Can I switch between weekly, monthly, and annual billing?
Yes — billing plans are managed per item through your dashboard. You can adjust the plan for any individual item at any time, giving you full flexibility.

Swap fee accounts

Creates the accounts that collect your swap fee, in bulk, without the Jupiter dashboard.
build: bulk-fee-accounts · 17 Sep

Each account costs about 0.002 SOL of rent (roughly 20¢), paid by the wallet you connect, and is recoverable later by closing it. Creating one changes nothing else: fees only start arriving once the account exists and the worker knows about it. Accounts already created are skipped.

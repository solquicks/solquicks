Swap fee accounts

Creates the accounts that collect your swap fee, in bulk, without the Jupiter dashboard.
build: bulk-fee-accounts · 19 Sep

Each account costs about 0.002 SOL of rent (roughly 40¢), paid by the wallet you connect, and you cannot get it back. Closing a referral token account is signed by the project admin — Jupiter's key, not yours — so treat this as money spent, not parked, and only create accounts for tokens people actually swap here. Every pair already earns a fee: one with no account pays it as SOL to the treasury instead. An account makes the fee come out of the swap itself, and keeps it working when the swapper is short of SOL. Accounts already created are skipped. Nothing needs adding to the code afterwards: the worker works out each fee account's address itself and starts using it within about 15 minutes of it appearing on chain.

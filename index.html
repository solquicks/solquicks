Reflect

Powered by Reflect. Non-custodial — your USDC moves straight from your wallet into the protocol, and we never hold it.

Your savings
Worth knowing before you deposit. The yield comes from lending your USDC to borrowers who put up more collateral than they take out. That is not risk-free: there is no insurance pool, and getting out depends on there being liquidity to get out into, which can tighten when markets are rough. Reflect has been audited by Offside Labs and Adevar Labs and publishes both reports. Only deposit what you could stand to be without.
